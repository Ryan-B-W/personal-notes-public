Dynabook

A concept by Alan Kay initially described as the KiddiComp in 1968 and later refined as the Dynabook in 1972. The basic idea of the Dynabook is a portable computer designed with children in mind that provides a computer in a notebook form-factor but still providing full-sized computer functionality wrapped up in a more intuitive interface.
