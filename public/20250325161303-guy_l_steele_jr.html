    Guy L. Steele Jr.

    Guy Lewis Steele Jr. is a computer scientist that has had an important impact on a verity of technologies. Most known for Lisp—and Scheme in particular—language designs, documentation and standardization; work in artificial intelligence; being the originator of Emacs with co-author David A. Moon; and work on the design and standardization of Common Lisp.
