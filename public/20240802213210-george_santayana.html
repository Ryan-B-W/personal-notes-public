    George Santayana

    Jorge Agustín Nicolás Ruiz de Santayana y Borrás, known in English as George Santayana, was an Spanish-American philosopher, essayist, poet, and novelist. Most known for a variety of aphorisms such as "Those who cannot remember the past are condemned to repeat it".
