    (opendyslexic.org) OpenDyslexic

    ROAM_REFS: https://opendyslexic.org/

    OpenDyslexic: A typeface for Dyslexia OpenDyslexic is a typeface designed against some common symptoms of dyslexia. If you like the way you are able to read this page, and others, then this typeface is for you! OpenDyslexic is free to use: The newest version of OpenDyslexic now uses the SIL-OFL license, giving you freedom to use it for personal use, business use, education, commercial, books, ebook readers, applications, websites, and any other project or purpose you need.
