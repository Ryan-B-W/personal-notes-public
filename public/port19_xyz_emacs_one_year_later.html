(port19.xyz) 💻 Emacs: One year later

ROAM_REFS: https://port19.xyz/tech/emacs1y/

On the 7th of January 2022 I decided to install Doom Emacs. I toyed with the basics that night and continued to do so the next day. After two months, I wrote a first blog post on it. That being the oldest post hosted on this site today (check it out). I stayed on Emacs for a while, switched back to Neovim for a few months and then came back again. I even wrote my first scientific paper in some org-latex hybrid. Orgmode, not Emacs, has given me great power which comes with great responsibility that I'm getting tired off.
