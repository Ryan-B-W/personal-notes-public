    Douglas Engelbart

    American inventor, engineer, and computer science pioneer. Known for inventing the computer mouse, pioneering early forms of graphical user interface (GUI), working on hypertext, and his famous "The Mother of All Demos" demonstration of the oN-Line System (NLS) in 1968.
