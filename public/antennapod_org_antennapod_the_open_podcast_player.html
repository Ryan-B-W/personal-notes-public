(antennapod.org) AntennaPod – The Open Podcast Player

ROAM_REFS: https://antennapod.org/

The Open Podcast Player AntennaPod is a podcast player that is completely open. The app is open-source and you can subscribe to any RSS feed. AntennaPod is built by volunteers without commercial interest, so it respects your privacy while giving you full control.
