    (wiki.gentoo.org) ARM hardware list - Gentoo wiki

    ROAM_REFS: https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/ARM_hardware_list

    This is a list of ARM hardware owned by people in the and channels have running Gentoo. Feel free to add to the list!

    The hardware probe system may be used to provide detailed hardware information, by uploading a probe using the available tool, and providing a link to that probe along with the entry here (see entry by Jens3 for an example).
