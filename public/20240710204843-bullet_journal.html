    Bullet Journal

    The Bullet Journal is an organizational method revolving around a bulleted list method of note taking in a journal with an index, daily logs, and any combination of other sections including but not limited to future log, monthly log, weekly log, collections, and any number of other custom additions.

    Some aspects of the system have passing similarities to the Zettelkasten method.
