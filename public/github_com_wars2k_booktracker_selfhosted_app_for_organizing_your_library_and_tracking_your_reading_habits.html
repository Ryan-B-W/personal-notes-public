    (github.com) wars2k/booktracker: Selfhosted app for organizing your library and tracking your reading habits.

    ROAM_REFS: https://github.com/wars2k/booktracker

    Booktracker is an open source application for managing and tracking a personal book library. Using metadata grabbed from Google Books, easily add books and organize them into collections. Set each book’s status, give it a rating, and track how long it took to read.
