    (www.canoeicf.com) Difference Between Canoe and Kayak - Olympic Sports

    ROAM_REFS: https://www.canoeicf.com/canoe-kayak-difference

    What's the difference between a Canoe and Kayak? Well the difference is relatively simple; it’s related to athlete’s position in the boat and the type of paddle they use to propel the boat. In a kayak, the paddler is seated and uses a double-bladed paddle pulling the blade through the water on alternate sides to move forward. In a canoe, the paddler kneels and uses a single-bladed paddle to propel the boat forward.
