    You might have heard of org-mode headers IDs. By default, these are Universally Unique Identifiers ( UUID s). In this post, I want to talk about what they are, why I use them (and you should, too), and how to make them into slugs — human-readable IDs that make sense. This will be a bit of a long explanation of what I discovered in org-mode, so buckle up..!
