Nix

The Nix package manager. Uses the Nix configuration language to manage packages and system or user configurations. Inspired the BSD ports system. Purports to provide a fully reproducible means of configuring systems. Primarily used by NixOS. Works on top of most major operating systems including Linux, *BSD, Mac OS, and Windows (via WSL2).
